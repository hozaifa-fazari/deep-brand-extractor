# Track B — Personal Branding & Client Targeting Plan

Goal: a positioning, an offer, a priced package, and a repeatable way to reach the people who
buy it. Estimated 7–9 exchanges.

## Coverage ledger

1. The offer
2. ICP (ideal client profile)
3. Pain
4. Positioning & category
5. Pricing & floors
6. Channels
7. Content pillars
8. Outreach
9. Objections, capacity & goal

---

## Question bank

Two per turn. Adapt to `PROFILE`.

### 1. The offer
- If you had to sell **one** productised thing — fixed scope, fixed price — what is it?
  *(named offers get referred; "I do video" does not)*
- Exactly what lands in the client's hands, and by when?
- What's explicitly **not** included? *(scope creep is a pricing problem)*
- What's the smallest version you'd sell, and the largest?

### 2. ICP
- Which industry buys this most easily today?
- Company size and rough budget band.
- Who signs — job title, not department.
- Geography and language. Any market you refuse?
- If unsure: *"Describe the best client you've had. We'll reverse-engineer the profile."*

### 3. Pain
- What is broken for that client right now, in their words?
- What does doing nothing cost them — money, time, reputation, deadline?
- What have they already tried that failed? *(this is the sharpest copy angle you'll get)*

### 4. Positioning & category
- Finish: "I'm the person you call when ______."
- What category do you want to own — and who currently owns it?
- Who do you get compared to, and how do you want to be different?

### 5. Pricing & floors
- Current rate, and how you charge — hourly, per project, per deliverable, retainer.
- **The floor you will not go below**, and the last time you broke it. *(the floor is the
  whole plan; everything else is negotiation)*
- Three tiers: what would you put in good / better / best?
- Deposit terms, revision limits, rush fee.

### 6. Channels
- Where does the ICP actually spend attention — not where it's easy to post.
- Where has real work come from in the last 12 months?
- Any channel you're currently pouring effort into with nothing to show?

### 7. Content pillars
- Three to five topics you can post about for a year without getting bored.
- What do you genuinely disagree with, that most people in your field believe? *(a real
  contrarian view outperforms tips)*
- Realistic cadence — be honest, not aspirational.
- Which format do you actually enjoy making?

### 8. Outreach
- Inbound, cold, referral, or platform — which are you willing to do?
- Which case study makes the strongest opener for this ICP?
- What's your warmest existing list — past clients, DMs, email?

### 9. Objections, capacity & goal
- Top three reasons prospects say no.
- Your current rebuttal for each. *(if there isn't one, that's the finding)*
- How many projects can you take per month at full quality?
- The 90-day goal, in a number.

## Document blueprint

1. **Positioning statement** — plus the "I'm the person you call when…" line
2. **Productised offer** — name, scope, deliverables, exclusions, turnaround, tiers
3. **ICP profile card** — industry, size, budget, title, geography, where they gather
4. **Pain/gain map** — their pain → your mechanism → their outcome
5. **Pricing table** — tiers, rates, deposit, revisions, rush fee, and **the floor in bold**
6. **Channel plan** — ranked, with the one to drop
7. **Content pillars & 30-day calendar skeleton** — pillar, format, cadence, 10 seed topics
8. **Outreach scripts** — cold DM, cold email, follow-up, referral ask; written out in full
   and ready to send
9. **Objection-handling table** — objection, root cause, response
10. **90-day roadmap** — milestones, KPIs, capacity ceiling
11. **Next actions** — 5–8 ordered tasks, owner, effort
